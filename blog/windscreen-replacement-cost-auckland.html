Windscreen Replacement Cost in Auckland

What truck owners actually pay for windscreen replacement in Auckland — and what drives the price up or down.

Consensus Pricing for Truck Windscreens in Auckland

Based on what Auckland truck glass specialists charge for standard truck windscreen replacement in 2025, most jobs fall between $250 and $500 when paying privately (not through insurance). This is the consensus range for a typical single-piece truck windscreen on common makes like Isuzu, Hino and Fuso.

Your actual price may vary depending on several factors:

  • Truck make and model: Common Japanese trucks (Isuzu, Hino, Fuso) usually have readily available glass and cost less. European trucks (Volvo, DAF, Mercedes) or rare models can cost more due to specialist glass sourcing.
  • Single vs split windscreen: Many trucks have a split windscreen (two pieces). Two-piece replacements naturally cost more than a single piece.
  • ADAS cameras: If your truck has a forward-facing camera for lane-keep or emergency braking, the windscreen replacement may need recalibration after fitting. This adds to the cost.
  • Heated or sensor-equipped glass: Heated windscreens, rain sensors and light sensors require specialised glass that costs more than basic laminated glass.
  • Mobile service: Most Auckland truck glass companies include mobile fitting in the price, but some may charge extra for travel to outer suburbs.

How to Get a Fair Estimate

The best way to get an accurate price is to call with your truck's make, model, year and registration number. A good glass specialist will confirm the exact glass type and give you a firm price before booking the job — not a vague range that changes on the day.

At Auckland Truck Glass, we give you a firm price when you call. We also handle insurance claims directly, which usually means you only pay your excess (often $0-$300) rather than the full replacement cost.

💡 Tip: If you're paying privately, ask whether the price includes GST, mobile fitting, disposal of the old glass, and any cure-time guidance. A low headline price that excludes these can end up costing more.

What About Side and Rear Glass?

Side window replacement for trucks typically ranges from $180 to $350, while rear or back glass is usually $200 to $400. Heated rear screens and wiper-fitted units sit at the higher end of that range.

These prices are for standard truck glass. If you have a custom tint, privacy glass, or a bonded panel that requires specialised removal, the price may increase.

Insurance vs Private Payment

If you have comprehensive commercial vehicle insurance, your windscreen replacement is almost certainly covered. We deal with AA, State, AMI, Vero, FMG, Tower and most other NZ insurers. We handle the claim, invoice them directly, and you only pay your excess.

Not insured? No problem — we give you a fair, fixed price with no hidden charges. Call 09 972-1091 for a firm estimate on your truck.
